# **Voizzy Master Plan (MVP)**

## **🧩 Core Problem**

Solopreneurs and small businesses struggle to collect and showcase testimonials easily. Most tools are either too complex, too expensive, or require clients to sign up.

## **🎯 Solution**

Voizzy is a simple, branded testimonial collection tool with a shareable form and embeddable wall of love. Clients submit text-based testimonials with a star rating — no login required.

## **🚀 MVP Goals**

Focus on simplicity and speed of use. Collect high-value feedback, display it beautifully, and upgrade users based on limits and branding needs.

## **✅ Core Features**

* Public form branded as "Voizzy" for Free & Basic users

* Collect text-only testimonials with a star rating (1 to 5\)

* Dashboard to manage testimonials: approve, reject, delete, or update status

* Dashboard stats: Total, Approved, Pending, Rejected

* One testimonial form per user

* iFrame embed bubble widget showing only approved testimonials

* Simple email/password sign-up and login

## **👤 Plans**

* **Free**: Max 3 testimonials, no form customization, Voizzy-branded form, Wall of Love bubble widget with approved testimonials

* **Basic ($19/mo)**: Unlimited testimonials, still Voizzy-branded, no form customization, Wall of Love bubble widget with approved testimonials.

* **Premium ($49/mo)**: Everything in Basic \+ Customize form color \+ Upload logo \+ Remove Voizzy branding

## **✂️ What We’re NOT Building (Yet)**

* Audio/video testimonials

* AI summarization

* Multi-form support

* Analytics or CAPTCHA

* Email notifications

## **🧠 Admin Role (For You)**

* Simple admin panel to view users, see number of testimonials, see their plans, manage flags/issues if needed

## **🛠️ Stack**

* **Frontend**: Next.js \+ TailwindCSS

* **Backend & DB**: Supabase (PostgreSQL \+ Auth \+ RLS)

## **🔄 Validation Path**

* Launch free plan with text testimonials only

* Collect usage data \+ user feedback

* Start monetizing via Basic and Premium tiers

* Iterate based on what users request most
